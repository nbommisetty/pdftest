2. Technology and Integration Fit Questions
This section focuses on the technical architecture of the XYZ platform and its flexibility to integrate with your bank's existing ecosystem.

Platform Architecture and Customization:

Describe the "Engagement Banking Platform" architecture. Is it a monolithic application or a microservices-based, composable platform?

What is the "buy-and-build" model, and how does it work in practice? Can we, as the bank, build our own features on top of the XYZ platform?

You mentioned Web/Mobile SDKs and a Design System. Can you provide a detailed overview of these tools? What technologies are they based on?

How can our internal technology team, using their own developers and technologies, build custom web components or micro-frontends and seamlessly integrate them into the XYZ experience?

What is the process for deploying custom features or extensions to the platform? Do these need to be reviewed or certified by XYZ?

Integration and Data:

Can we integrate with third-party fintechs and other providers through the XYZ platform? Do you have an existing marketplace of pre-built connectors?

What is your approach to data? How does the platform unify customer data from various sources to enable a single customer view and personalized experiences?

Does the platform provide an event-driven architecture, and can we subscribe to events from the XYZ platform to trigger actions in our own systems?

3. Commercial and Legal Questions (Intellectual Property and Licensing)
This is a critical area to ensure your bank maintains control over its proprietary digital experiences and intellectual property (IP).

Customization and IP: If we build a custom feature or journey using the XYZ platform and SDKs, who owns the intellectual property of that code? Will XYZ have any rights to use, license, or sell that same code to other clients?

Licensing Model: How is the XYZ platform licensed? Is it a per-user, per-feature, or subscription-based model? What is the cost structure for adding new lines of business or custom functionalities?

Source Code Access: Will the bank have access to the source code for the XYZ platform, or is it a black-box system? If not, what is the process for debugging or troubleshooting issues within the platform?

Exit Strategy: What happens if the bank decides to move away from the XYZ platform in the future? What is the process for migrating our data and any custom-built features? Are there any restrictions or costs associated with this process?
